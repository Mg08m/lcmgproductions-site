The studio

Made by someone who watches the sport.

LCMG Productions is an independent studio. The person behind it played club rugby in England and was brought up on hurling: both parents are from Limerick, and two nephews play the game today. The last match watched from the stands was Limerick against Cork in the Munster qualifier, a great weekend settled by a last-minute penalty. That is the moment these games are built around.

Realism, but fun. Nothing gets signed off unless it feels fun on a pad. Phase one is the sports the big publishers have left under-served. Phase two is broader. Hurling lives in the last puck. Rugby lives in the dark arts. Each game is its own game, made for people who watch the sport.

Privacy

What this site collects.

Nothing on the page itself. There are no cookies, no analytics, no forms and no third-party scripts or fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, or take part in a playtest, we keep your name, your email address and what you tell us for as long as we are working together, and only to reply to you and run the playtest. We do not sell or share it, and mail is handled by Google Workspace. Ask at the contact address above to see or delete what we hold. The data controller is LCMG Productions.
